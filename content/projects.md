A selection of things I have shipped and things I am building. Some are work, some are bets.

## Bugzy
**WhatsApp AI gut health coach** · at The Good Bug

An agentic health coach on WhatsApp built with RAG, multi-turn memory and hallucination guardrails. Scaled to **200K+ users in 6 months** with a 1.5x retention uplift. The hardest part was multi-turn memory on a channel with no session concept, which we solved with a summarise-and-tag memory layer plus a 200+ scenario eval set.

## Goodsy
**Agentic customer experience system** · at The Good Bug

A multi-step agent with tool-use, memory and human-in-the-loop escalation. It processes refunds, modifies orders and changes subscriptions. Autonomously resolves **65% of 24,000 monthly tickets**, saving INR 3CR+ in annual support opex. Shipped with a tiered autonomy model: small refunds the agent handles directly, larger ones queue for human confirmation.

## Face Shape AI
**Eyewear recommendation engine** · at Lenskart

A GPT-4 powered recommendation engine combining face-shape detection with prompt chaining. Evaluated on 500+ test cases and lifted conversion from 3% to 7.5% (**2.5x**) within 3 months.

## Apli.ai
**Hiring platform** · co-founded, acquired

An NLP-based job-matching engine built as a ranking problem with A/B tested scoring. Grew to **1,200+ institutions**, 6.5L+ candidates and 400+ companies in 18 months, then acquired by Edulab.

## CallHome
**AI voice companion for elderly parents** · side project

Daily outbound Hindi AI calls to elderly parents of NRI families, with WhatsApp summaries and a weekly digest. Stack: Sarvam AI, VAPI, Claude API, Exotel, Supabase, WhatsApp Business API.

## Sparq
**Kids AI companion** · side project

A dual-account kids AI app (parent B2C + school B2B), built to COPPA/FERPA/UK Children's Code standards. Stack: React Native/Expo, Supabase, Claude Sonnet, DALL-E 3, Next.js.

## Mimic
**macOS desktop agent** · side project

A macOS agent that passively observes screen workflows and automates them. Thesis: AI workflows break at the UI/prompt layer, and the fix belongs at the OS level. Applied to YC; continuing to build.

## Nexhumans
**AI-native post-purchase ops** · consulting

An AI-native post-purchase operations agency for Indian D2C brands, priced on outcomes.
